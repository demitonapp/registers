TMR and TfNSW obligations: protections in Demiton

TMR is Queensland's Department of Transport and Main Roads. TfNSW is Transport for New South Wales. Their instruments govern civil road jobs in those two states: the TMR Transport Infrastructure Contract (TIC) is the contract form itself, and the six specifications are what the principal calls up onto a job. In the obligation library these enter through two of the seven sources: contract (the TIC) and principal (the specifications).

How an obligation becomes a protection

Each obligation names one duty, cited to its clause, and carries two independent tags: a protection type (the engine that runs it) and a disease (the loss it guards against). An obligation only runs as a protection once it is switched on for a project and the register behind it has data. Rows whose evidence is record_in_demiton or register run today; rows marked not_collected have an engine but no field or system feeding them yet.

EngineWhat it does
WatchWaits for the event that opens a window, then tracks the deadline (EOT claim, latent-condition notice).
CountdownCounts to a fixed date, flagged at 90, 30 and 7 days out (final claim after practical completion).
CheckLooks for evidence that must exist, on a cadence (daily inspection, as-constructed records).
ThresholdTracks a measure against the line it must not cross (compaction at or above a minimum).

The five diseases, in research-cost order: Rework, Baseline overrun, Forfeited entitlements, Disputes, Non-compliance. The type and the disease are set separately: the same watch can guard forfeited entitlements or disputes; what decides the disease is what the missed duty costs.

2. Baseline overrun in the app: overrun_signal 0 obligations from TMR or TfNSW

Neither TMR nor TfNSW publishes a baseline-overrun duty, so none of their 23 obligations guard this disease. The overrun protection today is Demiton's own labelled default, overrun.budget_tolerance.au and .nz, at 0% against a job budget, not a principal-imposed duty.

Relatively trivial to add

Nine obligations are already transcribed, cited and engine-routed, but not_collected. They split by what is missing. The first two buckets are cheap: the register exists and only a field or a computation is missing. The third needs a new register or a producer feeding an existing one.
